# Dashboard redesign

Mode is Operate. The user delegated the design and explicitly requested no questions. This is a code-led replacement of the previous green sidebar identity.

## Direction candidates

1. Market disclosure factsheet. White, graphite, blue data, tight comparison columns.
2. National statistics release. Neutral hierarchy, open charts, compact footnotes.
3. Scientific journal figures. Restrained axes, precise legends, adjacent measurement summaries.
4. Public infrastructure identity. Strong route color with clear station-like labels.
5. Electricity settlement statement. Tabular alignment and compact source references.
6. Weather service forecast bulletin. White plotting field, blue forecast, amber retrospective observations, a side column of measurements.
7. Engineering standards publication. Dense sans typography and disciplined section spacing.

The direction roll assigned candidate 6, seed:cc18daf2dda59235. The forecast bulletin supports the existing data model without inventing financial signals. Split-flap and cassette challengers lose audience identification and product clarity through costume. Timetable and Japanese density are competitive on scanning but lose clarity for this small dataset. Streaming and botanical challengers lose both axes. Their useful discipline is consistent alignment and a complete visual grammar; no motifs are borrowed.

## Direction contract

THESIS. Make the next-day profile the first thing an analyst reads. Replace the wide green sidebar and oversized metric strip with horizontal navigation, an open chart, and a compact measurement column.

OWN-WORLD. White working surface, graphite ink, cool gray subdivisions, cobalt forecast and amber actuals. One DM Sans family, clear rectangular controls, subtle 4px corners, no decorative badges or rounded chart cards.

STORY. Select the purchase date and cutoff, inspect the delivery curve, enable hindsight, then verify exact hourly evidence. Accuracy keeps the same hierarchy.

FIRST VIEWPORT. A 64px horizontal header holds the wordmark, three navigation buttons, and archive identity. The centered workspace has a compact title and export action, then a filter toolbar. The chart occupies the left three quarters; stacked measurements occupy the right quarter. The curve and its whole axis fit in a normal desktop viewport. Tables follow directly below.

FORM. Weather service forecast bulletin, candidate 6. seed:cc18daf2dda59235. Signature interaction is the measured sliding unit selection paired with softly changing values. Motion remains limited to state feedback and respects reduced motion.
